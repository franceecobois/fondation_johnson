Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 1, 2026

1. Introduction

The JOHNSON Foundation attaches paramount importance to the protection of its participants' personal data. This policy transparently describes how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our Wheel of Generosity game.

By submitting the prize claim form, you accept the practices described below.

2. Data Controller

The data controller is the JOHNSON Foundation, whose registered office is located at [full address]. For any question relating to your data, you may contact us by email at privacy@fondationjohnson.online.

3. Data Collected

As part of participation and the handover of prizes, we collect the following information:

  • Identity: last name and first name(s)
  • Contact details: email address, phone number, city of residence, full postal address
  • Socio-demographic data: nationality, profession (optional)
  • Draw result: prize awarded by the wheel

This information is collected exclusively through the form voluntarily filled in by the winner. No other data is collected automatically (IP address, tracking cookies, etc.).

4. Purposes of Processing

Your data is used only to:

  • Manage your participation in the game and validate the draw
  • Contact you to organize the handover of the prize won
  • Maintain an internal register of awarded donations
  • Respond to any requests or complaints

No automated decision-making or profiling is carried out based on this data.

5. Legal Basis

Processing is based on your explicit consent when you tick the form submission box (or by the very act of submission). It is also necessary for the performance of the terms of use that you have accepted.

6. Recipients of the Data

Your data is strictly confidential and is only accessible to authorized members of the JOHNSON Foundation responsible for managing donations. It is never sold, rented, or shared with third parties for commercial or advertising purposes.

We may be required to disclose certain information if the law obliges us to do so (judicial authorities, tax administrations).

7. Retention Period

Winners' data is retained for a period of 12 months after the actual handover of the prize, for follow-up and evidentiary purposes. After this period, it is permanently deleted from our systems, unless otherwise required by law.

8. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures (encryption of transmissions, access restricted by authentication, firewalls) to protect your data against loss, unauthorized access, disclosure, or destruction.

9. Your Rights

In accordance with applicable regulations, you have the following rights:

  • Right of access: obtain communication of the data concerning you.
  • Right to rectification: correct inaccurate information.
  • Right to object: object to processing on legitimate grounds.
  • Right to erasure: request the deletion of your data within legal limits.
  • Right to portability: receive your data in a structured format.

To exercise these rights, simply send an email to privacy@fondationjohnson.online or a postal letter to our registered office. A response will be provided to you within 30 days.

10. Changes to the Policy

We reserve the right to update this policy. Any changes will be published on this page and, if substantial, indicated by an informational notice on the site.
